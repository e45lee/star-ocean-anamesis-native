# Plan: English text in the 3.7.0 client (draft for review)

An initial plan built on the findings in [english.md](english.md), for the user to review. Nothing here is implemented yet. Each step lists its effort (S = a day or less, M = a few days, L = a week or more), what it depends on, how it is proved and where it sits on the server-first rule ([AGENTS.md "Hard rules"](../AGENTS.md#hard-rules)).

## Shape

- **One switch, per server.** `--lang ja|en` is a `ServerConfig` flag for `soa` and `soa-server` (`server/include/soaserver/cli.h`). It defaults to `ja` until the user decides otherwise (Q5).
- **The English goes only into what the client is served:**
  - the served master's `ja_` rows (a `ClientMaster` hook);
  - the edited Scenario files and other members (a CDN overlay);
  - the server's own texts.

  The server's own rules keep reading the Japanese master. They match Japanese text in places: `kDefaultEventKeywords`, and the present lines composed from master templates.
- **Mixed by design.** A row without English keeps its Japanese. Every text source is recorded per row in a coverage report.
- **Generated, not committed.** The English master and Scenario files are generated at server start from `data/basmaster-gl.sqlite3`, which is already in git, plus a small committed table of our own fixes. The game's files with our text in them stay out of git, as the served master does today.

## Questions for the user

- **Q1. Which content first?** The UI and system texts (E1–E3, E6: small, about 39% of the master's rows), or the story (E4–E5: EP1 is fully covered, EP2 and EP3 are almost entirely Japanese)? The proposed order is UI first.
- **Q2. The gaps:** leave them Japanese (mixed text), or fill them? 30,560 master rows have no Global counterpart and 15,980 were never translated, plus most of EP2, EP3 and the later events. If filled, by machine translation, clearly marked as such in the coverage report and overridable?
- **Q3. Fan translations:** are there any you want imported (the wiki's names in `soa_save/names_en.json`, or fan story translations)? They are out of scope until you add them; E8's import path takes them.
- **Q4. Images:** are English replacements for text in images in scope (home buttons, footer, banners, tutorial pages)? They would be our own art, like the stand-ins, since Global's are lost. Or should images stay Japanese?
- **Q5. The default:** should `--lang` default to `ja` (sessions and gates unchanged; English opt-in), or to `en` for release packages only?
- **Q6. Client changes:** are word wrap (E10b) and the hard-coded strings (E10a) worth a client change, or should we keep server-only and pre-wrap (E7)?
- **Q7. Global's older English for edited JP texts:** 821 rows translate an older JP text, e.g. old hit counts in factor descriptions. Take them anyway (slightly wrong numbers), or leave them Japanese (proposed)?
- **Q8. Wording:** keep Global's terms ("Gems" for 紋章石, "FOL", "Augment" for 進化, "Transmute"), or prefer different ones?
